## Interface captures

`editor-select.png`, `editor-transform.png`, `editor-array.png`, `editor-contour.png`, `editor-catalog-blueprints.png`, `editor-outliner.png`, and `editor-settings.png` were captured from the automated BuildWorks 0.19.33 Unity Workbench. They show real implemented UI states, but they are not evidence of behavior inside Valheim.

In-game images of F9, the indexed hammer, and sequential construction should be added only after the 0.19.33 manual smoke test, so public documentation does not preserve screenshots of known defects.
